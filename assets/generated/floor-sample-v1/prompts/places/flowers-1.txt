The place: the flower fields. Far right: a tall cream windmill with a terracotta cap and four lattice sails, standing on a hill, its sails high in the picture. Far left: a steep sage-green hillside covered with long curved stripes of pink, yellow, lilac and white flowers, with one large round tree at its top and a small cream farmhouse with a terracotta roof beside it. Between them low rolling hills striped with flowers, paler with distance.
