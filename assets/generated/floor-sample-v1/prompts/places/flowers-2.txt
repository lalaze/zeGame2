The place: the flower fields. Far left: a tall cream windmill with a terracotta cap and four lattice sails on a hill. Far right: a steep hillside of flower stripes in pink, yellow, lilac and white, a glass greenhouse with a white frame on it, two round blossoming trees, and a smaller second windmill further up behind them. Between them low rolling sage-green hills striped with flowers, paler with distance.
