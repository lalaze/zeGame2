The place: the seaside, seen from a harbour town. Far left: the tall red-and-white striped lighthouse at the end of a stone mole. Far right: a steep cluster of pastel harbour houses (cream, pale pink, pale blue) with coral roofs, climbing the hill, with two leaning palms at their foot and the masts of moored sailboats with cream furled sails before them. Between them the calm teal sea to a straight horizon a little above the middle, a far island and a small sail.
