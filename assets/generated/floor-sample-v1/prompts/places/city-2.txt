The place: the city. Far left: an elegant old clock tower of cream stone with a green copper roof (a plain clock face with two hands and no numerals) above brick roofs with skylights and roof gardens. Far right: three pale blue-grey high-rise towers of different heights with rows of little windows, one with a round water tank on top and one with a red-tipped mast, above a brick block with striped awnings on its roof terrace. Between them a soft, hazy, lower skyline and an elevated tram line with a small honey-yellow tram.
