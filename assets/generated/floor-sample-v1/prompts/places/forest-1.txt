The place: the mountain woods. Far right: a log cabin with a steep roof lightly dusted with snow and a smoking stone chimney, standing high on a slope among tall dark-green firs. Far left: a group of very tall firs of different heights in front of a steep snow-capped blue-grey peak. Between them lower, paler wooded ridges and a second snowy range far away, with a little mist in the valley.
