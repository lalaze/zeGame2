The subject: the café itself as a tiny house cut open at the front like a doll's house: a pitched terracotta tiled roof with a small chimney, cream walls, and two warm lamp-lit rooms one above the other (upstairs a round table with a cup and a hanging lamp; downstairs a sage-green counter with a cake stand and a hanging lamp), and a small cream and sage striped awning over the ground floor. Keep the rooms very simple: three or four big shapes each. The background: one soft, pale sky blue.
