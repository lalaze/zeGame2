Hot drinks come in the café's cream ceramic cups on matching saucers, like the cups on the ground floor's shelf; iced drinks in clear glasses.
1. Espresso: a small demitasse of dark espresso with golden crema on a saucer.
2. Americano: a tall cream mug of black coffee.
3. Latte: a wide cup of milky coffee with a leaf drawn in the foam.
4. Cappuccino: a cup with a tall dome of milk foam dusted with cocoa.
5. Flat white: a small cup of light-brown coffee with a little heart in the foam.
6. Cortado: a short clear glass, coffee below and a layer of warm milk on top.
7. Mocha: a mug of chocolate coffee topped with whipped cream and a chocolate drizzle.
8. Viennese coffee: a clear glass mug of dark coffee under a high swirl of whipped cream.
