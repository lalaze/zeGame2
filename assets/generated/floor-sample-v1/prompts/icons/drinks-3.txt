Hot drinks come in the café's cream ceramic cups on matching saucers, like the cups on the ground floor's shelf; iced drinks in clear glasses.
1. Lemon black tea: a clear glass of iced amber tea with a lemon slice on the rim.
2. Chai latte: a cup of spiced milky tea with a cinnamon stick and a star anise.
3. Mixed fruit tea: a small clear glass teapot of red tea with orange, apple and strawberry pieces inside.
4. Hojicha latte: a cup of light roasted-brown tea latte with soft foam.
5. Matcha latte: a cup of pale green matcha latte with a leaf drawn in the foam.
6. Caramel frappé: a tall clear cup of blended iced coffee topped with whipped cream and caramel, with a straw.
7. Strawberry smoothie: a tall glass of pink smoothie with a strawberry on the rim and a straw.
8. Royal milk tea: a cream teacup with a thin gold rim, full of creamy milk tea.
