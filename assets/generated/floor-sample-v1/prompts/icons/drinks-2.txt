Hot drinks come in the café's cream ceramic cups on matching saucers, like the cups on the ground floor's shelf; iced drinks in clear glasses.
1. Caramel macchiato: a clear glass with layers of milk and coffee, caramel drizzled over the foam.
2. Cold brew: a tall clear glass of dark iced coffee with ice cubes.
3. Dirty coffee: a short clear glass of cold white milk with a dark espresso shot just poured on top, swirling in.
4. Pour-over: a cream ceramic dripper sitting on a small glass carafe of coffee.
5. Sea salt cream coffee: a cup of coffee under a thick pale cream cap with a few salt flakes.
6. Affogato: a small glass bowl with a scoop of vanilla ice cream and espresso poured over it.
7. Earl Grey tea: a cream cup of clear amber tea on a saucer with a small sprig of lavender beside it.
8. Hot chocolate: a mug of hot chocolate topped with mini marshmallows.
