Cells 1 to 5 are five tiny café buildings seen from the front, one for each place a café of the chain can stand, each on a small patch of its own ground, with a blank signboard, a striped awning, a door and warmly lit windows, as in image 3:
1. The old town café: cream plaster under a terracotta tiled roof with a chimney, a dusty-red and cream striped awning, ivy at the corners, two potted plants, on cobblestones.
2. The seaside café: white clapboard under a soft-blue roof, blue shutters, a blue and cream striped awning, a red-and-white lifebuoy on the wall and two wooden barrels, on sand.
3. The mountain woods café: a log cabin with a snow-covered roof and a smoking stone chimney, a small snowy fir beside it, on snow.
4. The city café: a tall, narrow two-storey red-brick building with a flat roof and stone cornice, a sage-green striped awning, a street lamp beside it, on paving.
5. The flower fields café: a half-timbered cottage with a thatched honey-coloured roof and a little windmill behind it, a white picket fence and a row of pink and red tulips in front, on grass.
6. A map pin: a round-headed terracotta-red pin with a cream centre, pointing down.
