1. The book nook: tall wooden bookshelves full of colourful books along the back wall on both sides of a bay window with checked ochre curtains, two soft armchairs, a reading lamp and a round rug.
2. The glasshouse: a sage-green iron and glass back wall with trees showing behind it, potting shelves with rows of potted herbs and ferns, hanging planters, a wooden bench and a watering can on a pale green tiled floor.
3. The garden patio: an open-air terrace under an oak pergola hung with string lights, a low stone wall with planter boxes of pink and cream flowers along the back, lemon trees in pots at both sides, a garden bench on terracotta paving.
4. The clay studio: a workroom with two windows, shelves of drying cups, bowls and jugs in cream, sage and terracotta, a potter's wheel with a half-made pot, a pegboard of tools and a small kiln.
5. The star tea room: a cozy evening room in deep teal with two big windows showing a starry night sky and a crescent moon, a brass telescope on a tripod by the window, a low tea table with a teapot and cups, floor cushions and a row of tea tins on a shelf.
