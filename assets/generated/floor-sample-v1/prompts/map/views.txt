1. The seaside: a calm teal sea to the horizon with a far island, a red-and-white striped lighthouse on grey rocks at the right, a little sailboat with a cream sail, a sandy beach along the bottom with a red-and-cream parasol and a towel, and a leaning palm framing the left edge.
2. The mountain woods: snow-capped blue-grey peaks behind a wooded ridge, a green meadow with a log cabin whose chimney smokes, its roof dusted with snow, and tall dark-green firs framing both sides.
3. The city: a skyline of pale blue-grey towers, nearer blocks with rows of windows, warm brick buildings framing both sides, and a honey-yellow tram running along an avenue with two street lamps and two small round trees.
4. The flower fields: rolling sage-green hills with one round tree, a cream windmill with a terracotta cap and four sails at the right, and below it long stripes of pink, yellow, lilac and white flowers widening towards us, with a low wooden fence along the bottom.
