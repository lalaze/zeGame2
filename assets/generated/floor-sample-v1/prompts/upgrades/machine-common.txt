Image 1 is the ground-floor counter of this cozy café game: on it, behind the cake display, stands a grey steel espresso machine with three groups and white cups on top. Image 2 is the game's approved art direction. Paint the same machine after three upgrades: THREE espresso machines side by side in one row on flat, even pure magenta (#FF00FF), with clear magenta gaps between them, no shadow on the magenta. Each is seen from exactly the same angle as in image 1 (from the front and a little above), in exactly its style (soft hand-drawn, thin warm-brown outlines, gentle low-saturation colours, warm light from the upper left), standing level on its own flat bottom edge, white cups with saucers in a row on top. All three have the same width, about 1.45 times their height with the cups. Each must look clearly richer than the one before, so a player sees the change at a small size on a phone. No letters, no words, no numbers, no counter, no people. Landscape image.
