The rooftop terrace, open to the sky: soft blue sky with clouds and neighbouring rooftops behind, a low brick parapet around the back and sides, string lights on posts, a cream parasol, two small round tables with chairs, potted herbs, lavender and hydrangeas, one cat asleep on a round cushion and another sitting on the parapet; on the right, the little door of the stair hatch where the staircase arrives. The roof deck is the floor slab at the bottom; there is no ceiling.
