Left building: the bookshop. Two storeys under a dark slate roof with two small chimneys, deep teal-green painted woodwork with cream plaster upstairs, two upstairs windows with flower boxes of green herbs. Downstairs a big shop window full of bookshelves with colourful book spines and a reading lamp glowing among them, and a wooden door with glass panes on the right.

Right building: the plant shop. A low single-storey glasshouse shop, lower than the bookshop: a sage-green iron frame with a hipped glass roof and two small finials, the signboard under the glass roof, the door with glass panes in the middle, and tall windows on both sides full of potted plants, hanging ferns and trailing ivy on shelves.
