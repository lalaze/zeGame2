Left building: the tea shop. Two storeys under a green tiled roof with upturned eaves and two small chimneys, cream plaster with dark sage-green woodwork, two upstairs windows. Downstairs a rolled bamboo blind over a wide shop window with a teapot, a branch in a vase and three tea caddies, and a wooden door with glass panes on the right.

Right building: an empty shop waiting for a new owner. Two storeys of warm red brick with cream stone trim and a stepped parapet instead of a roof, three small upstairs windows. Downstairs a rolled-up honey-yellow awning over two large, bare, dark shop windows with nothing behind them, and a plain wooden door in the middle. No lamps lit, no flowers: tidy but empty.
