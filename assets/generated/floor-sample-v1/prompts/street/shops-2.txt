Left building: the flower shop. Two storeys under a terracotta tiled roof with two small chimneys, dusty-pink plaster with cream stone trim, two upstairs windows with flower boxes of pink blossoms. Downstairs a cream scalloped awning over the whole front, a wooden door with glass panes in the middle, and a window on each side with buckets of pink, cream and lilac bouquets.

Right building: the pottery. Two storeys under a dark slate roof with two small chimneys, honey-yellow plaster in a dark timber frame, two upstairs windows. Downstairs a short terracotta-red awning over a wide shop window with shelves of glazed pots, bowls, jugs and cups in cream, sage and terracotta, and a wooden door with glass panes on the right.
