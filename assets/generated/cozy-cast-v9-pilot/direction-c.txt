STYLE DIRECTION C - taller townsfolk
Do NOT use the big-head chibi build of image 2. Draw grown-up townsfolk about 3.5 to 4 heads tall: small head, visible shoulders, longer legs, and a different build for each character (row 1 slim, row 2 tall, row 3 sturdy). Faces are two dark pixel eyes and nothing more than a hint of blush. The look of villagers in a classic 16-bit life-sim, as original characters, lit and coloured to match image 1. Glasses are a thin one-pixel frame with no lens glare.
