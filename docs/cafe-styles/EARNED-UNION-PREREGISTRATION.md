## Selection and stronger acceptance, fixed before outcomes

Primary metrics are net-liquid profit (higher), observed-by-cutoff loss rate (lower), waste cost (lower) and terminal latest50-review rating (higher). Served throughput remains supporting only. Payback remains first own-capital recovery with null right-censoring.

A favorable comparison uses the existing paired t29 descriptive95% interval wholly in the favorable direction and mean improvement at least1 unit and1% of the absolute control mean for profit/waste,0.005 absolute loss rate, or0.05 stars. Loss advantages must additionally survive the conservative bound in which all garden pending guests fail and all comparator pending guests succeed. Preserve original and sensitivity results together. No multiplicity-corrected or universal-superiority claim is made.

For each garden menu, compare against every OTHER policy in the35-policy table, including other garden menus and plain controls. It qualifies for training selection only if:

1. Its focus improves at least one primary metric materially over its own exact-menu plain control.
2. Against every other policy, at least one primary metric is materially favorable, or all primary seed-level outcomes are exactly equivalent (numerical tolerance1e-8). A failure to reach significance is not treated as proof of nondominance.
3. At least one other-focus policy has a material advantage over garden on a primary metric, documenting a real operating tradeoff rather than a claimed universal best style.

If multiple garden menus qualify, choose greatest training mean net-liquid profit, then lexicographically smallest menu ID. Freeze that menu and every comparator edge: use the first favorable metric in fixed priority net-liquid profit, loss rate, waste, rating. Exact equivalents are labeled separately. For the headline, use that same metric priority and the strongest other-focus comparator on that metric among the favorable edges (highest comparator profit/rating, lowest loss/waste; lexicographic ID tie). Freeze a downside using priority loss rate, waste, rating, profit, then the strongest other-focus comparator on that downside, with the same tie rule.

Holdout must confirm the own-plain primary benefit, EVERY frozen favorable comparator edge, and the frozen material downside using the same thresholds and directions. Frozen equivalent policies must remain equivalent. Do not switch the selected metric or comparator after seeing holdout. This deliberately demanding criterion establishes a robust sampled niche, not merely a favorable pair against an underperforming route.
