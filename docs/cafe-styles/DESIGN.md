# Six styles: design

Implemented candidate, with independent source review and focused regression gates passed; final full-matrix acceptance remains pending. Route selection is guidance and a bounded operating choice, not an irreversible class. Assets and underlying existing systems remain available when mixing styles. Any economic modifier must have a visible cost and mechanism; no arbitrary benchmark-winning rewards.

## Audited operating contract (first candidate)

Only one focus is active. No focus preserves legacy behavior; all existing investments remain mixable and can be used under any focus. Focus changes only during preparation, with no cash payment, grant, permanent buff or claimable reward. Stage goals recognize real retained assets and actual delivered portions. Missing legacy handoff counts start at zero, never invented from total visits.

- Coffee: coffee preparation takes 12% longer; delivered coffee earns a 4% standard, 8% skilled or 12% signature premium. Real proficiency and paid research matter. Non-coffee remains unchanged.
- Bakery: with a kitchen, drink/bakery pairing grows from 35% to 50%; cook station work takes 10% less time, while walking remains physical. Extra portions consume ingredients and preparation; unused baked stock still goes stale. Before kitchen, improve espresso margin and save 800 for kitchen, then 30 for roll plus stock.
- Takeaway: drink-only orders with total base preparation at most 3 seconds use the counter; qualifying orders get a 6% discount. Slow drinks and mixed bakery orders use ordinary service. No magic increase to queue length.
- Cats: seated handoffs with comfort at least 5 gain 4% per cared-for cat (maximum 12%); those guests stay four extra seconds per active cat. Existing adoption costs and ongoing care still apply; no supplies means no cat bonus. Improve menu margin before adoption.
- Garden: comfort at least 5 plus a genuinely placed plant or equipped plant sill enables tea table service. Delivered seated tea gets 6%; actual tea/pastry pairs receive 12% instead, not both. Eligible pairing is 50%, dining preference 80%, dwell six seconds longer. Ingredients, both preparations and cross-room delivery remain real costs.
- Community: at comfort at least 5, servers walk 15% faster and table patience gains 10 seconds, but arrivals fall 10%. Ratings still require actual food quality and timely delivery. Longer patience cannot erase a slow-service rating.

Garden identity begins with tea and planted surroundings in the original café. Room order stays reading → greenhouse → terrace/garden. The existing tutorial is separate optional guidance, not a required route path.

## Evidence requirements
Bonuses conditional on a table use actual handoff context, never a blanket catalog premium. Regular-guest order substitution happens before qualifying the final order. Forecasts and simulation share pairing/station calculations. Actual table, fast-drink, cared-cat and tea/pastry handoffs are recorded once at charge and survive saves, without synthesizing legacy history. No choice multiplies another style's special modifier; ordinary tips, paid recipe levels, VIP behavior, research, cats and promotions retain their existing independent systems.
