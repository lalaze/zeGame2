## Garden interpretation without changing the frozen test

No policy clearly dominates garden across the earned-union samples. The selected black-tea/scone policy is descriptively mean-nondominated among all 35 policies in both training and independent holdout. This is a bounded sample result, not proof of universal superiority.

The same preselected benefits and costs reproduce:

- Own-menu plain net-profit gain: training **171,098** coins, paired 95% interval **[115,339, 226,856]**; holdout **263,823 [222,046, 305,601]**.
- Frozen same-menu bakery waste advantage: training **362 [334, 390]** fewer wasted coins; holdout **337 [310, 364]** fewer.
- Holdout own-plain cost: **1.30 percentage points** more lost demand **[0.86, 1.75]**, and **0.293 fewer stars [0.187, 0.398]**.
- All 30 selected garden holdout runs recover their paid capital without cash-constrained restocking.

**Auxiliary failure retained:** garden's frozen profit comparison with same-menu cats is **+23,924 [−29,614, 77,462]**, with 19/30 positive seeds. Its interval includes zero. This is neither a garden win nor proof of equality. The other 33 frozen edges pass, but the conjunctive all-34 gate does not. The favorable descriptive cats-waste comparison is not substituted for the frozen profit metric. See the [unchanged selection](results/earned-union-v1-training-selection.json) and [failed holdout verification](results/earned-union-v1-holdout-verification.json).

The user's requested reproducible advantage with an operating cost is supported without claiming victory over every rival. ## Complete pressure findings

All **2,100** feature/rush samples are complete: [raw results](results/earned-union-v1-pressure.json), [summary](results/earned-union-v1-pressure-summary.json), [whole-policy audit](results/earned-union-v1-pressure-descriptive-audit.json), and [frozen-edge verification](results/earned-union-v1-pressure-verification.json). Independent checks reproduce every setup, seed, clock, accounting identity and frozen comparison. Every one of the 70 policy cells has a positive operating-profit lower 95% bound; the smallest is **601,259 coins**. No sample has a cash-constrained restock or failed promotion booking.

Garden recovers capital in **30/30 samples at each pressure**. Its own-plain net-profit benefit remains **215,591 [165,162, 266,019]** with feature traffic and **131,896 [75,471, 188,320]** under rush. Costs increase: own-plain loss rises **2.19 pp** with feature traffic and **4.55 pp** under rush; rating falls **0.455** and **0.481** stars respectively.

**Cats has a real rush profit advantage over garden on the same tea/scone menu: 59,746 coins [14,500, 104,993].** This is stronger than the inconclusive base-pressure difference and must not be called a tie. Feature reproduces all 34 frozen edges; rush reproduces 33/34 and fails that cats-profit edge. Garden retains a frozen bakery-waste advantage under both pressures, but is not the rush-profit winner. The combined strict-edge flag remains false; the separately specified pressure sustainability check passes.

Positive operations are not universal capital recovery: **736/1,050 feature** and **1,036/1,050 rush** samples recover their full shared portfolio within the clock. The rest are explicitly right-censored, not granted future revenue.

## Retained limitations

- The stronger all-competitor profit/frontier gate fails one frozen edge in base holdout and one under rush. Its failure is reproducible, preserved and not repaired by changing metrics.
- Garden's originally underdeveloped nine common-asset policies remain weak. The earned-union panel tests a paid alternative strategy with more productive recipes and staffing; it does not rewrite the original results or establish universal optimality.
- Fixed-window positive operations do not mean every portfolio pays back by the cutoff, nor prove unlimited-horizon sustainability. Mean nondominance is not a significance-based guarantee.
- The native hidden-tab check is inconclusive in the available cloud-browser iframe. The ten real-source/real-Sim pause regressions are not a successful native-browser timing smoke; mobile viewport coverage is not physical-device coverage.
